Jess Li — Design System v1.0

The system behind the work.

One cohesive language for every page, component, and interaction. Monochrome, typographic-first, and quietly precise. This document is the single source of truth for tokens, type, color, spacing, components, and motion.

01Brand

Calm, premium, and unmistakably deliberate.

01
Typography is the interface
Hierarchy is built with weight, scale, and space — not decoration.
02
Monochrome discipline
One ink, one paper, graded greys. Color is reserved, never decorative.
03
Space is a material
Whitespace does the work most borders and boxes try to do.
04
Motion with a reason
Every animation reveals content, gives feedback, or shows state.
05
Detail earns trust
Alignment, optical spacing, and consistency are the whole point.
06
One playful signature
The cursor-tracking eyes are the single moment of warmth. Just one.
Personality

Editorial and considered, like a well-set magazine. Confident without shouting. Precise without being cold. A designer who sweats the small type.

Tone of voice

Plain, specific, and honest. Short sentences. Real numbers over adjectives. Says the caveat out loud. Never uses filler verbs or hype.

Motion philosophy

Slow in, settle gently. Reveals over reactions. Nothing loops for attention. Everything respects reduced-motion and collapses to still.
